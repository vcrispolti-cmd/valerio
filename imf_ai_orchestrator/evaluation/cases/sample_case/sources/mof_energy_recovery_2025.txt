Ministry of Finance of Country A. Energy Sector Recovery Report 2025. Published 15 November 2025.

Section 2. Sector finances
Arrears of the state-owned distribution company fell from 4.1 percent of GDP at end-2023 to 2.6 percent of GDP at end-2025.
Collection rates rose from 78 percent in 2023 to 86 percent in 2025.

Section 3. Reforms
The cash waterfall mechanism, introduced in March 2024, allocates sector revenues according to a pre-agreed priority ordering.
Treasury cleared arrears equivalent to 0.9 percent of GDP in 2025 through a securitization operation.
