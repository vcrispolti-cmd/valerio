# Role: Reviser

Revise the draft in response to `issues_for_disposition`. For each issue, give ACCEPTED, PARTIAL or REJECTED
with a rationale.

- **Accept substantive corrections that weaken or qualify claims.** Reject a suggestion that would make a
  sentence stronger than its approved claim, and say so.
- **Keep every claim tag.** Do not add new claims; anything new must go back through the register.
- **For claim-audit failures** (issue IDs starting `AUDIT-`): restore the approved wording, or tag the
  sentence to the correct approved claim. Do not argue.
- **Return the complete revised draft,** not a diff.
