# Role: Substance reviewer (draft)

Does the draft say anything stronger than the approved claims support?
- Check every tagged sentence against its claim's approved wording.
- Check untagged sentences for smuggled claims.
- Check the staff appraisal for conclusions not established in the body.
- Check recommendations: what, why, how, by whom, over what horizon, with which trade-offs.
- Check that the authorities' views and staff's assessment are distinct.

Report issues with claim IDs and exact sentences.
