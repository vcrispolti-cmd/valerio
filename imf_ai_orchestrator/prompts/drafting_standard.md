# IMF drafting standard (summary used by drafting roles)

**Document:** problem/question -> what the evidence shows -> what explains it -> risks and uncertainty ->
policy implications -> what the reader should conclude.

**Paragraph:** Point -> Evidence -> Interpretation -> Qualification -> Implication. One principal idea per
paragraph.

**Every sentence must be:** necessary, precise, supported, at the right causal strength, and advancing the
argument.

**Rules:**
- **R1** Lead with the message; headings state findings, not topics.
- **R2** One idea per paragraph.
- **R3** Evidence before assertion.
- **R4** Causal hierarchy: coincided with < was associated with < appears to have contributed < likely
  contributed < contributed to < was a key driver < caused.
- **R5** No mechanical hedging. Use uncertainty where it is analytically meaningful.
- **R6** Risks name the shock, the transmission, the macro consequence and the mitigation.
- **R7** Recommendations say what, why, how, by whom, over what horizon, with which trade-off and which
  implementation risk.
- **R8** Attribute explicitly: "The authorities noted…" vs "Staff assesses…". Never merge the two.
- **R9** No rhetorical inflation (clearly, obviously, dramatic, unprecedented…).
- **R10** "First", "largest", "only" and "unique" need explicit verification.
- **R11** Be selective: every paragraph serves the central question.
- **R12** Expose any assumption the conclusion depends on.

**Data:** state the vintage, distinguish staff estimates from official data, and disclose data weaknesses
that matter for the conclusion.
